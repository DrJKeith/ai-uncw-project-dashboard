# AI Hub and GAABS Public Progress Update

Reporting period: July 18-21, 2026

## Executive Summary

The AI Hub and GAABS remain in a foundation-building and delivery-readiness phase. Near-term work is concentrated on completing and testing Summer AI Institute programming, advancing the established governance path, maintaining responsible public-guidance boundaries, and participating in a scheduled cross-system discussion about institutional AI readiness. This update does not represent a broad service launch.

## What Changed

- The final-day Summer AI Institute hands-on workshop remains an active preparation item. Preparation is not delivery; completion requires a tested participant path, safe scenario, facilitator guidance, and a no-login fallback.
- A July 22 discussion with Axim is scheduled around a UNC System-wide initiative focused on helping universities become AI-ready and proficient. The meeting is a coordination and learning opportunity, not evidence of a UNCW commitment, delegated authority, or approved implementation plan.
- Late-July delivery and institutional-service obligations continue to constrain capacity. New commitments should remain bounded and sequenced behind scheduled Institute work.

## Current Priorities

1. Complete and test the Summer AI Institute hands-on workshop.
2. Prepare for the July 22 cross-system AI-readiness discussion and capture its scope, participants, decision rights, expected contributions, and follow-up.
3. Advance the AI advisory-framework memo through the established senior-review path without implying that membership or invitations are approved.
4. Maintain the AI@UNCW and ITS guidance feedback cycle as review, not approval.
5. Confirm the Digital Consultation Lab schedule and shared access workflow.
6. Protect readiness for the scheduled August 5 Advancement follow-up.
