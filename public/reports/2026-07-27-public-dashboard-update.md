# AI Hub and GAABS Public Progress Update

Reporting period: July 22-27, 2026
Report date: 2026-07-27
Source basis: Project-file-only

## Executive Summary

The AI Hub and GAABS continue a foundation-building and delivery-readiness phase. The AI Hub website is scheduled to launch July 27. Separately, the weekly AI Hub discussion identified a practical adoption-support opportunity: create or locate concise guidance that helps people use ChatGPT EDU licenses efficiently and responsibly, then determine whether an educational agent would provide meaningful additional value. This update does not claim a broad service launch, measured return on investment, or an effectiveness result.

## What Changed

- The AI Hub website is scheduled to launch July 27. A public launch check will identify any material issue or held content after launch.
- The AI Hub is scoping a knowledge article on efficient, responsible ChatGPT EDU use. The intended contribution is practical user support and license value.
- A companion educational agent is under consideration. No agent build, policy, or effectiveness claim is approved by this update.

## Current Priorities

1. Verify the public AI Hub website launch and capture any material issue or correction owner.
2. Complete and test the Summer AI Institute hands-on workshop.
3. Scope the ChatGPT EDU use-support article: audience, priority behaviors, authoritative sources, ownership, and maintenance.
4. Determine whether an educational agent adds value beyond the article before committing to build it.
5. Maintain the established governance and guidance-review boundaries.

## Workstream Updates

- **Staff and Faculty Development:** A role-aware ChatGPT EDU use-support article is being scoped as practical adoption support. The possible educational agent remains a planning concept.
- **Communications:** The AI Hub website is scheduled to launch July 27; public verification follows launch.
- **Research:** Descriptive adoption monitoring remains distinct from an effectiveness study. No causal or ROI claim is added.

## Milestones

- **July 27:** Verify the public AI Hub website launch after it occurs.

- **July 28-30:** Deliver Summer AI Institute programming after readiness requirements are met.

## Risks, Blockers, and Dependencies

- **Medium:** Website launch content could be mistaken for approved policy or broad service availability. Mitigation: retain review and ownership boundaries and correct material issues promptly.
- **Medium:** An educational agent could duplicate static guidance or imply endorsement before its purpose and maintenance model are clear. Mitigation: establish the article baseline, audience, source ownership, and agent-specific value before building.
- **High:** The completed EDU-license rollout could be misrepresented as proof of effectiveness or return on investment. Mitigation: describe this effort as practical support and descriptive institutional learning only.
