## Executive Summary

AI Hub and GAABS continued foundation-building across institutional capability, student learning, and community engagement. Confirmed progress includes establishing AI Currents as a UNCW-wide Viva Engage discussion community; creating GAABS SharePoint and semester booking surfaces, pending end-to-end verification; a positive tool-sharing exchange with Towson University colleagues; and faculty-support office hours with John Holsten. These statements establish activity and reported progress, not participation levels, outcomes, effectiveness, partnerships, service commitments, or live-system verification.

## Confirmed Developments

- AI Currents has been established as a UNCW-wide Viva Engage discussion community. No membership, engagement metrics, outcomes, moderation arrangements, or continuing-support commitments are claimed.
- GAABS SharePoint and semester booking surfaces have been created, pending end-to-end verification of scope, routing, privacy, permissions, maintenance ownership, and user experience.
- A positive tool-sharing exchange occurred with Towson University colleagues. It is an exchange, not a partnership, transfer agreement, endorsement, or continuing commitment.
- Faculty-support office hours were held with John Holsten. This establishes a consultation activity, not learner outcomes, course changes, a standing service, or continuing support commitment.
- A strategic discussion with Peter Kalish and Parker Moran developed a proposed three-pillar frame: targeted administrative AI capability, student AI-skill development, and community-facing business applications. The group hopes to bring the frame to the AI Hub Advisory Board when it convenes.
