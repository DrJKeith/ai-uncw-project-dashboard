# AI Hub and GAABS Public Project Progress Report

**Reporting period:** Spring-Summer 2026 through July 17, 2026  
**Report date:** July 17, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Julian Keith, Ph.D., AI Strategic Lead, UNCW  
**Public contact:** [AI@UNCW](mailto:ai@uncw.edu)  
**Source basis:** Project-file-only. This report summarizes project records through July 17, 2026; entries that still need confirmation are labeled explicitly. It is not a live email, calendar, or Teams verification.

## Executive Summary

The AI Hub and the Generative AI Agent Builders Studio (GAABS) have moved from initial authorization into foundation-building and governed execution. The AI Hub coordinates UNCW's AI activities, resources, communication, and institutional direction; GAABS is its advanced applied tool-building arm. Since spring 2026, the initiative has established an operating model, advanced governance and public-guidance work, created faculty and staff development activities, developed a responsible tool-showcase concept, confirmed a shared Library consultation space, and prepared major Summer AI Institute programming. The immediate priority is durable institutional capacity, clear governance, tested programming, and responsible public communication before broader service expansion.

## What Changed Since the June Progress Report

- Senior leadership confirmed a single, small AI Hub Advisory Board with spokes and existing or emerging working groups. The next governance deliverable is a formal Chancellor memo establishing the board and authorizing the invitation process.
- The Library confirmed the Digital Consultation Lab as a shared, Library-facilitated space for GAABS, GIS, and data consultations, supporting both drop-ins and scheduled meetings.
- Two Summer AI Institute presentations were completed: an AI Hub / GAABS introduction and “How I Trained My Dragon,” a session on creating custom GPTs for courses.
- The remaining major Summer AI Institute deliverable was narrowed to a tested, one-hour hands-on agent-building workshop with participant activities, facilitator guidance, and a no-login fallback.
- Practical staff capability-building advanced through a July agent-building session with University Advancement and a confirmed August 5 follow-up.
- Governance and public-communication safeguards were strengthened so that draft guidance, advisory-board planning, and showcase materials are not represented as approved, final, or broadly available before the appropriate review.
- The team project-monitoring dashboard was built and launched for shared monitoring on July 17.
- A fruitful discussion with Michelle Lashley of Elon University opened possible UNCW-Elon exchanges through talks, workshops, and related activities; these remain opportunities to scope, not formal commitments.
- The completed EDU-license rollout was not coordinated or communicated as a systematic rollout and study. It is therefore not study-ready for an effectiveness claim; current reporting must distinguish descriptive adoption monitoring from causal/effectiveness evaluation.
