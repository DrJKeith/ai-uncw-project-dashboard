# AI Hub and GAABS Student Consulting Pilot Update

Reporting period: July 27, 2026
Report date: 2026-07-27
Source basis: Project-file-only

## Executive Summary

The AI Hub and GAABS are planning an explicitly experimental student consulting pilot with graduate students in Data Science and AI. The pilot is intended to create supervised practical-build experience through a real institutional use case, progressive skill development, and structured reflection. It is not yet a production service or a commitment to broad client availability.

## What Changed

- A phased student consulting pilot was agreed in principle, using paid graduate student roles and peer learning to support knowledge transfer.
- Azure AI Foundry and Codex are the intended tools, pending access, centrally managed funding, and student-account confirmation.
- An internal form-guidance and validation use case is being considered as the first pilot project. Its scope, data/rule ownership, and accuracy tests must be established before implementation.

## Current Priorities

1. Confirm student access, centrally managed funding, and appropriate operating controls for the intended tools.
2. Finalize student selection, orientation, training, supervision, and recurring team meetings.
3. Scope the first internal form-guidance pilot with authorized information sources, validation criteria, and a maintenance plan.
4. Preserve a knowledge-transfer plan as student roles change during the academic year.

## Workstream Updates

- **Infrastructure:** A supervised graduate-student build-capacity pilot is being planned; platform access and operating controls are prerequisites.
- **Student AI Literacy:** The pilot will use progressive demonstrations, practical project work, peer learning, and end-of-semester reflection to build student capability.

## Milestones

- **Before pilot kickoff:** Confirm access, funding, student appointments, and training cadence.
- **Pilot planning:** Establish the internal form-guidance use case, authorized source material, validation plan, and maintenance owner.
- **End of semester:** Student teams present process, lessons learned, successes, failures, and post-mortems.

## Risks, Blockers, and Dependencies

- **High:** Tool access, funding, or student-account readiness may delay the pilot. Mitigation: confirm these prerequisites before student work begins.
- **High:** Student turnover can leave a prototype without a maintainer. Mitigation: pair newer and more experienced students and document handoff responsibilities.
- **Medium:** A pilot can become too technically ambitious for its client need or cohort readiness. Mitigation: favor maintainable solutions and confirm scope before implementation.

## Decisions or Support Needed

No new public decision or support request is proposed. The pilot remains contingent on access, funding, scope, validation, and continuity conditions.

## Status Boundaries

- This update is project-file-only and is not a new live verification of tool access, funding, student appointments, or implementation readiness.
- The pilot is planned and experimental; it is not a production consulting service or a completed delivery.
- The initial use case is under scope review; no deployment, validated accuracy result, or client-facing availability is claimed.
- Student learning and practical use are distinct from institutional endorsement of any specific tool for every context.
